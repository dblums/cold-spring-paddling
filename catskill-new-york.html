        Important note

        Tides and currents are predictions, not observations. Wind, waves, runoff, storm surge and other factors can affect actual conditions. Weather is sourced from the National Weather Service. Use your own judgment and paddle at your own risk.

        Current from NOAA station ACT3781_1. Flood and ebb headings are the river’s own axis here. Tide from NOAA station 8518974 at Hudson, about five miles upriver. Both are predictions, baked into this page and good through 31 Dec 2028; values between NOAA’s published points are interpolated. Weather and alerts come live from the National Weather Service grid covering Catskill. Water temperature comes from NOAA Turkey Point, 12 miles downriver, or NOAA Coxsackie when that gauge is out. Sun and moon are computed rather than fetched, and agree with the US Naval Observatory to within a couple of minutes.
