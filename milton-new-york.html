        Important note

        Tides and currents are predictions, not observations. Wind, waves, runoff, storm surge and other factors can affect actual conditions. Weather is sourced from the National Weather Service. Use your own judgment and paddle at your own risk.

        Current from NOAA station HUR0620_11 (Mid-Hudson Suspension Bridge), about three miles upriver, at the 16 ft bin nearest the surface. Tide from NOAA station 8518945 at Poughkeepsie, about three miles upriver. Both are predictions, baked into this page and good through 31 Dec 2028; values between NOAA’s published points are interpolated. Weather and alerts come live from the National Weather Service grid covering Milton. Water temperature has no nearby sensor — it is estimated between NOAA The Battery, downriver, and NOAA Turkey Point, up. Sun and moon are computed rather than fetched, and agree with the US Naval Observatory to within a couple of minutes.
