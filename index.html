Constitution Marsh is a 270-acre tidal wetland that you can access by paddling under the Metro-North train trestle just south of Cold Spring — but only when there is enough vertical clearance to do so safely.

    Important note

    These are predictions, not observations. They do not include wind, waves, barge traffic, upstream rain runoff, or storm surge — any of which can change conditions. Use your own judgment!

    Current from NOAA station ACT3726_1 (West Point, off Duck Island) — about a mile and a half downriver, so it is used unshifted. Tide from NOAA station 8518934 (Beacon), shifted 30 minutes earlier to approximate Cold Spring. Values between NOAA's published slack/max and high/low points are interpolated; predictions run through 31 Dec 2028. The ±2h trestle and mud rules are local rules of thumb — look at the clearance yourself before you commit, and remember the tide inside the marsh lags the river.
