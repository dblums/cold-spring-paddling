        Important note

        Tides and currents are predictions, not observations. Wind, waves, runoff, storm surge and other factors can affect actual conditions. Weather is sourced from the National Weather Service. Use your own judgment and paddle at your own risk.

        Current from NOAA station NYH1928_12 (Hudson River, Pier 92), a third of a mile from the piers, at the 6 ft bin nearest the surface. Tide from NOAA station 8530645 at Weehawken, a mile across the river. Both are predictions, baked into this page and good through 31 Dec 2028; values between NOAA’s published points are interpolated. Weather and alerts come live from the National Weather Service grid covering Midtown Manhattan. Water temperature comes from NOAA The Battery, 4 miles downriver, or NOAA Turkey Point when that gauge is out. Sun and moon are computed rather than fetched, and agree with the US Naval Observatory to within a couple of minutes.
