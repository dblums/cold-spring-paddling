        Important note

        Tides and currents are predictions, not observations. Wind, waves, runoff, storm surge and other factors can affect actual conditions. Weather is sourced from the National Weather Service. Use your own judgment and paddle at your own risk.

        Current from NOAA station HUR0614_9 (Hudson), about a mile and a half from the launch, at the 14 ft bin nearest the surface. Tide from NOAA station 8518974, which is Hudson itself, so it is used unshifted. Both are predictions, baked into this page and good through 31 Dec 2028; values between NOAA’s published points are interpolated. Weather and alerts come live from the National Weather Service grid covering Hudson. Water temperature has no nearby sensor — it is estimated between NOAA Turkey Point, upriver, and The Battery, down. Sun and moon are computed rather than fetched, and agree with the US Naval Observatory to within a couple of minutes.
